# Basic language and length checks

Basic is written for approximately fifth-grade comprehension: short sentences, common words, immediately explained essential terms, and one causal step at a time. It is adult-facing educational speech.

The table reports **Flesch–Kincaid grade estimates** calculated with textstat 0.7.13 and the CMU pronunciation dictionary, with the library's fallback for unlisted words. The body score excludes the fixed opening, fixed closing and question headings; it includes medicine names and any retained medical terms. The full spoken score includes everything that will be spoken. No drug-name substitution was used to lower either score. Proper names and essential safety terms can raise the score.

Readability formulas do not test understanding, accuracy, pronunciation or how well a viewer follows an animation. Editorial checks also consider explanations, sequence and unnecessary jargon. No user comprehension testing or independent clinical validation is claimed. The unchanged closing contains terms above fifth-grade level; its wording is retained as requested.
