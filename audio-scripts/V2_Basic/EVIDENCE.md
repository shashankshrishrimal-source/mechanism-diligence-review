# V2_Basic — evidence and adaptation record

**Not narration.** The source of this adaptation is [V2_Scientific](../V2_Scientific/V2_Scientific.md), with its [dated evidence companion](../V2_Scientific/V2_Scientific_Evidence.md). Product/route locks, source IDs, named papers and label sections remain in that companion. Sources were rechecked where the adaptation retained safety and dose statements; the [shared adaptation record](../V2_Adaptation_Evidence.md) identifies those checks and any clarifications.

## How to map a claim

| Adapted passage | Scientific evidence chapter to consult |
|---|---|
| Identity, selected product and route | Opening / What is this drug? and Route |
| Normal function and target interaction | Biological problem and Target |
| Changes after target interaction | System change |
| Benefit and harm | Benefit / harm, label warnings and contraindications |
| Known and uncertain mechanism | Established / uncertain |
| Duration and clearance | Duration / elimination |
| Dose context and restrictions | Dose and Must not overlook |
| Recap | Take-home and the supporting target/system chapters |

Each named drug's section gives its actual sources; source IDs are scoped to that drug. Use the per-drug adaptation notes for combined or simplified passages. Do not treat this mapping table as evidence for a claim absent from the source.

## Essential boundaries

The Basic version removes numeric schedules as a whole, rather than keeping a dose while losing adjustment criteria. Medical keeps the selected indication and formulation when stating a numeric regimen. Neither level converts plasma half-life into duration of benefit. Mechanistic shorthand may remove intermediate names but must preserve the direction and reversibility of the action. Human therapeutic uncertainty remains explicit. Formal contraindications, warnings and recommendations retain their different strength.

Independent clinical review remains pending. A readability score is a screening aid, not proof that a viewer understands a drug or can make treatment decisions. The [language check](../READABILITY.md) reports what was measured.
