Let’s talk about empagliflozin, which is the generic name.
Jardiance is a brand name, and it may be the name you already know.

What is this medicine?

This tablet can lower blood sugar in type two diabetes. It also helps some people with heart failure or long-term kidney disease. Those benefits are not just due to lower sugar.

How does it get to work?

The drug moves from the gut into blood, then reaches the kidneys. The kidneys filter blood. The filtered fluid enters tiny tubes. Sugar enters these tubes, but most normally goes back into blood.

How does it work?

A protein called SGLT2 helps return sugar to blood. It carries sugar and sodium together. Sodium is part of salt.

The drug binds to this protein and blocks its normal movement. Less sugar can pass back through it. More sugar stays in the tubes and leaves in urine. The protein is not destroyed.

That sugar holds water in the tubes, so more water leaves too. Changed salt flow also sends signals back to the kidney’s filters. This can reduce pressure within them. Changes in water and salt balance can ease work for the heart.

How can it help and cause harm?

Blood sugar may fall, and the heart and kidneys may benefit. Extra sugar in urine raises the risk of yeast infections around the sex organs. Losing too much water can cause low blood pressure.

Another risk is ketoacidosis. This means harmful acids build up in blood. It can happen even when blood sugar is not very high.

What is still unclear?

We know this drug helps selected people with heart or kidney disease. We do not yet know how much each change explains that help.

How long does it work?

Sugar loss and acid problems can last after treatment stops. A low drug level does not mean those risks have ended.

What should we know about doses and risks?

The tablet dose depends on the illness and the person. It is not recommended to control sugar in type one diabetes. Kidney function also affects whether sugar-control use is suitable.

Do not use it if allergic to an ingredient. If vomiting, belly pain, or trouble breathing occurs, stop it and get urgent care. These can be signs of acid buildup. Serious infections around the sex organs or in the urinary tract also need prompt care.

The label calls for a pause at least three days before surgery or tests that require a long fast, when possible. The care team guides the pause and restart.

What is the main idea?

The drug blocks sugar recovery in the kidneys. This changes sugar, salt, and water handling.
