Let’s talk about losartan, which is the generic name.
Cozaar is a brand name, and it may be the name you already know.

What is this medicine?

Losartan can lower high blood pressure. We’re following tablets taken by mouth for adults. One body signal tells blood vessels to tighten. It also tells the body to hold salt and water. Losartan blocks part of that signal’s effect.

How does it get to work?

The drug passes from the gut into the blood. The liver changes some of it into another active form. Both forms work. Blood carries them to cells in vessels and other parts of the body.

How does it work?

The signal is called angiotensin two. It binds to a protein on the cell’s surface. This protein receives the signal and passes the message inside. It is called a receptor.

The message makes calcium move inside vessel muscle cells. That helps the muscle tighten. Losartan binds to this receptor and blocks the message. It does not destroy the signal. The drug can let go again.

With less of the message getting through, vessels relax. Blood has more room to flow. The signal to hold salt also falls.

Losartan has a separate action in the kidneys. It blocks some recovery of a waste product called urate. More urate can leave in urine. This is not the same as blocking the blood-pressure signal.

How can it help and cause harm?

Lower pressure can help. Too much of a drop can cause dizziness. The changes can raise potassium, a mineral in blood. In some cases, kidney filtering can worsen. Blood tests help check these risks.

The drug can help some kidney problems, but it does not protect every kidney in every case.

What is still unclear?

We know the receptor block lowers blood pressure. We do not know if lower urate adds a separate heart benefit. Losartan is not approved to treat gout.

How long does it work?

In studies, much of the pressure effect appeared within a week. The largest effect sometimes took three to six weeks. The active form made by the liver also helps it last. Drug leaves through urine and stool.

What should we know about doses and risks?

A clinician chooses the dose for these oral tablets. Blood pressure, fluid loss, liver health, and other drugs matter. This film does not choose your dose.

Do not take it if you are allergic to it. Do not combine it with aliskiren if you have diabetes.

It can injure or kill an unborn baby. Stop it as soon as pregnancy is found and contact your clinician.

Potassium products and some pain drugs can raise risks. Have your medicines checked. Swelling of the face, tongue, or throat needs urgent medical help.

What is the main idea?

Losartan blocks a signal that tightens vessels. It also acts on a separate kidney protein. Each action has its own role and limits.
