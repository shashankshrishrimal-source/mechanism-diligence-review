# Esketamine

Welcome to Mechanism Diligence.
Let’s talk about esketamine, which is the generic name.
Spravato is a brand name, and it may be the name you already know.

## What is this drug?

Esketamine is one mirror-image form of ketamine. This film covers Spravato nasal spray for adults with treatment-resistant depression, meaning inadequate response to prior antidepressant treatment.

## What biological problem does it address?

Glutamate is a major excitatory messenger in brain networks. Its receptors include NMDA and AMPA receptors, which can act as ion channels. Depression is not established to be simply too much or too little glutamate.

## How does it reach its target?

Supervised nasal administration delivers esketamine across the nasal lining into blood. It then crosses the blood-brain barrier. This is systemic distribution, not a demonstrated direct route from the nose to a chosen synapse.

## What happens at the target?

Esketamine binds within the NMDA-receptor channel and reduces ion flow. It is a noncompetitive antagonist: it does not simply compete at glutamate’s own binding site.

This target interaction is established. The label states that the mechanism producing the antidepressant effect is unknown. Channel blockade is the first known step, not a complete explanation of clinical response.

## How does that action change the system?

One leading model proposes altered network activity followed by increased AMPA signaling and changes in synaptic plasticity, the ability of nerve-cell connections to change.

Growth-factor and protein-production pathways may participate. The complete sequence has not been demonstrated in people receiving nasal esketamine. It should not be described as proven repair or rebuilding of an individual’s synapses.

## What is established, and what is still uncertain?

Trials establish benefit for some people with treatment-resistant depression. They do not identify every necessary molecular step. A possible opioid-system contribution remains unresolved. Findings from intravenous mixed-form ketamine cannot automatically be assigned to nasal esketamine.

## How does the action connect to benefit and harm?

Changes in brain activity can accompany improvement and adverse effects. Dissociation, feeling disconnected from oneself or the surroundings, is not proof of benefit.

The boxed warning covers sedation, dissociation, respiratory depression, abuse and misuse, and suicidal thoughts and behaviors. The antidepressant suicide warning particularly concerns young adults; all patients require monitoring. Blood pressure can rise too.

## How long does the action last?

Trial groups showed differences in depression scores around twenty-four hours after treatment, without guaranteeing an individual response time. The liver metabolizes the drug. Its seven-to-twelve-hour terminal half-life does not determine the duration of benefit or maintenance schedule.

## What adult dosing does the label describe?

For adult treatment-resistant depression, the current label permits use alone or with an oral antidepressant. It describes fifty-six or eighty-four milligrams twice weekly for four weeks, weekly during weeks five through eight, then weekly or every other week according to response and tolerability. This is label information, not a personal dose.

## What must not be overlooked?

Do not use it with aneurysmal vascular disease, abnormal artery-to-vein connections, prior bleeding within the brain, or hypersensitivity to esketamine, ketamine, or ingredients. Each session requires a certified setting under REMS, the restricted safety program, and at least two hours of monitoring, including oxygen levels and blood pressure checks. Observation continues until clinically stable. No driving until the next day after restful sleep. It has not been shown to prevent suicide and does not replace needed hospitalization.

## What is the take-home mechanism?

Esketamine blocks NMDA-channel activity. Its complete antidepressant mechanism remains unknown. Keep established target action separate from proposed explanations of sustained improvement.
