## Ibuprofen: the essential inhibition sequence

Show arachidonic acid approaching and entering a free COX substrate channel. Show the COX conversion to PGG2 and the separate peroxidase step to PGH2; distinguish the downstream enzymes that make tissue-specific prostanoids. Then introduce ibuprofen and show **reversible, noncovalent contacts** in the channel that interfere with productive substrate access. Product formation decreases. Show dissociation as a separate event, after which access can resume. This is an explanatory molecular model, not a measured binding trajectory.

Do not show ibuprofen covalently attaching to COX, destroying the enzyme, irreversibly switching it off, or occupying every enzyme at once. Do not add extra protein subunits, stabilizers, ligands or an unsupported literal human atomic structure. The evidence companion identifies the limitations of the structural sources. Keep a consistent cast of labeled molecules across shots.

## Rules for every drug

- **1920×1080, full-bleed black, cinematic 3D.** Molecules visibly act. Generate clean plates with no letters or baked-in text; add every molecule's label afterward in that molecule's color. Keep labels readable and use names as well as color.
- Use one explicitly labeled route and product per film. Ibuprofen here is an immediate-release oral tablet with the adult **OTC Advil 200 mg** dose context. Do not import prescription-dose limits. Oral is not the default for the collection: albuterol is oral inhalation, adalimumab subcutaneous, and esketamine intranasal.
- Keep narration and picture synchronized. Mark changes of location and scale explicitly. Do not invent stomach passage, first-pass metabolism, blood travel, literal speeds, concentration ratios or atomic anatomy unsupported by the selected product and evidence.
- Repeat distinct target → action → consequence loops for multiple mechanisms. Distinguish downstream effects from additional direct targets. Keep supported benefit and harm from the same action in a shared frame; do not invent a single mechanistic explanation for every adverse effect.
- Retain scientific definitions and uncertainty. Mark a proposed pathway visibly for its full sequence and qualify it aloud as written. Keep animal-study detail in the evidence notes; do not add animal experiments or present laboratory hypotheses as established human biology.
- Keep onset, plasma half-life, duration of action and clinical response separate. Dose examples are bounded adult label information, not personal dosing instructions. If a needed label limit is absent, omit that line rather than calculate or invent a maximum. Do not soften contraindications.
- No mascot, patient face, joke, music under the science, commercial delivery or happy-ending flourish.
