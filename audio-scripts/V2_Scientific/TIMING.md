# Approximate Scientific video lengths

Planning estimates at about **120–130 spoken words per minute**, allowing modest pauses and visual holds. Questions, opening and closing are included. These are not measured film durations. Add time when a mechanism needs a longer visual explanation; do not pad or rush narration to fit these ranges.
