# V2_Scientific — Evidence and Review Record

**Not narration.** Research checked October 25, 2026. Companion to V2_Scientific.md.

The records identify the selected US product, route and adult dosing context, label version or revision where available, named scientific sources, claim-to-chapter mappings, substantive corrections, access limitations and filming cautions. A current posted label can have an older revision date. Retrieval does not imply a new FDA change or independent clinical approval.

This was a targeted narrative review with parallel research and cross-checks, not an exhaustive systematic review. Foundational mechanistic work is retained when it remains the best explanation. Newer research does not automatically replace established pharmacology. Human clinical findings, isolated human-protein experiments, other laboratory evidence, and unresolved hypotheses are distinguished. Animal-specific detail stays in these notes where needed to document a limitation; it is not narrated.

Use each chapter's source map to connect narration and visual claims to evidence. Recheck the exact product's live US label before recording; keep the dated source record. A concise safety segment is not the entire prescribing information. For atom-specific animation, use only the cited structure's supported components, species and experimental state. No source supplies literal molecule counts, animation speeds or whole-body scale continuity.

### Corrections and production checks

The revision replaces a simple “more serotonin fixes a deficiency” story with transporter inhibition followed by incompletely understood circuit adaptation. It explains what SERT physically does and why blocking it is different from activating a serotonin receptor. BDNF is defined and qualified rather than presented as a proved universal mediator. The 2024 rat slice study is not advertised as a newly established human sertraline mechanism.

The selected adult tablet dose is bounded to MDD; panic/PTSD/social-anxiety, PMDD, pediatric OCD, and solution-specific rules are not blended. The boxed warning is explicit, with the risk group distinguished from monitoring of all patients. Formal contraindications remain formal contraindications. Recheck current PI §§2, 4, 5 and 7 immediately before production; the concise narration is not an exhaustive safety list. Clinical review should also consider hyponatremia, angle-closure glaucoma, seizures, QT risk factors, and interacting drugs when selecting supplementary website material.

For animation: show a transporter alternating access across one membrane, then inhibited in an outward-facing state. Do not show a permanently plugged ion pore, serotonin pouring out of vesicles because of sertraline, every neuron being excited, or new synapses growing as a confirmed immediate human outcome. Blood concentration and symptom trajectories need distinct time representations.
