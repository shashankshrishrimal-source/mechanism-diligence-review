
# Amlodipine MOA Script — Educational Draft
# Product: Oral amlodipine besylate tablets (US label). Liquid products have different instructions.
# Scope: Adults with hypertension (primary use in this film); also labeled for angina.

## Chapter 0: Welcome / Title
Welcome to Mechanism Diligence. You might be here to understand a drug you were prescribed, or to learn how it works. This one is amlodipine. Norvasc is a brand name you may know. This film follows oral amlodipine besylate tablets. Education only, not personal medical advice. Independent clinical review is pending.

## Chapter 1: What is high blood pressure?
What is high blood pressure? High blood pressure means the force of blood against vessel walls stays too high for too long. You cannot feel the number. Only a cuff or monitor confirms it. Over years it strains the heart, vessels, and kidneys.

## Chapter 2: What does amlodipine do?
How does amlodipine lower pressure? Amlodipine is a dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker. It relaxes blood vessels so the heart pumps against less resistance.

## Chapter 3: The journey
It begins as a swallowed tablet. It is absorbed and reaches the blood vessels. The label says it acts directly on vascular smooth muscle.

## Chapter 4: The channel
Inside the vessel wall. Smooth muscle cells have L-type calcium channels. Calcium enters through those channels and helps the muscle contract. Amlodipine sits in the channel and slows that entry. With less calcium inside, the muscle relaxes. The vessel widens. Pressure falls. The effect on vessels is greater than the effect on heart muscle at usual doses. Onset is gradual because the drug binds and leaves the channel slowly.

## Chapter 5: What changes
Vessels relax. Blood pressure falls. The full drop on a monitor can take days to weeks. It works while you take it once a day. It does not repair existing vessel damage.

## Chapter 6: Dose
On the US label for adults with high blood pressure, the usual start is 5 milligrams once a day. The maximum is 10 milligrams once a day. Some people start at 2.5 milligrams. These are label limits, not a dose chosen for you. Take it with or without food.

## Chapter 7: Limits
Do not use amlodipine if you have had a hypersensitivity reaction to it. Swelling in the ankles or legs can occur and is more common at higher doses. Dizziness is possible, especially when standing. This film does not show your personal risk.
