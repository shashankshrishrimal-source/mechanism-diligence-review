
# Apixaban MOA Script — Educational Draft
# Product: Adult Eliquis oral tablets only (US label). Other formulations separate.
# Scope: Labeled uses for stroke risk reduction in nonvalvular atrial fibrillation; treatment and reduction of recurrence of DVT/PE; prophylaxis after hip or knee replacement.

## Chapter 0: Welcome / Title
Welcome to Mechanism Diligence. You might be here to understand a drug you were prescribed, or to learn how it works. This one is apixaban. Eliquis is the brand name. This film follows adult oral Eliquis tablets. Education only, not personal medical advice. Independent clinical review is pending.

## Chapter 1: What is a blood clot risk?
What is a harmful clot? In atrial fibrillation the heart can form a clot that travels. In the veins a clot can form in the leg and travel to the lung. You may not feel the clot forming. Tests or symptoms confirm it. A clot can block blood flow.

## Chapter 2: What does apixaban do?
How does apixaban reduce clotting? Apixaban is a factor Xa inhibitor. Factor Xa helps turn prothrombin into thrombin. Thrombin helps fibrin form a mesh that traps cells into a clot. Apixaban blocks factor Xa, so less thrombin is made and fewer clots form. It does not dissolve a clot that is already there.

## Chapter 3: The journey
It begins as a swallowed tablet. It is absorbed into the blood. There it meets factor Xa, both free in the blood and attached to a forming clot.

## Chapter 4: The block
Factor Xa sits in the clotting cascade. Apixaban binds it directly and reversibly. With less factor Xa activity, thrombin generation falls. Less fibrin forms. The clot is less likely to grow or start. Platelets still work; the drug does not block them directly.

## Chapter 5: What changes
The risk of certain clots falls while you take it. The effect continues only while the medicine is on board. Stopping suddenly can raise the risk of a clot. It works for the labeled uses while taken as directed.

## Chapter 6: Dose
On the US label for nonvalvular atrial fibrillation the usual dose is 5 milligrams twice a day. Some people take 2.5 milligrams twice a day based on age, weight, or kidney function. Other uses have their own dose plans. These are label directions, not a dose chosen for you. Take with or without food.

## Chapter 7: Limits
Do not use if you have active bleeding or a severe hypersensitivity to apixaban. There are boxed warnings about stopping it early and about spinal procedures. Bleeding can be serious. This film does not show your personal risk.
