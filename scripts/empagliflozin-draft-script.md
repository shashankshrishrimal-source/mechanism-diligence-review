# Empagliflozin (Jardiance) MOA Script

Scope: US single-ingredient Jardiance (empagliflozin) oral tablets. Three labeled adult uses named separately. Education only.

## Spoken Script

Welcome to Mechanism Diligence. You might be here to understand a drug you were prescribed, or to learn how it works. This one is empagliflozin. Jardiance is the brand name. This film follows oral tablets. Education only, not personal medical advice.

What are the labeled uses?
The label has three adult plans. One is to improve blood sugar control in type 2 diabetes, with diet and exercise. One is to reduce the risk of cardiovascular death and hospitalization for heart failure. One is to reduce the risk of further kidney decline, end-stage kidney disease, cardiovascular death, and hospitalization in adults with chronic kidney disease at risk of progression. These are separate labeled uses. Not every person qualifies for every one.

How does it work in the kidney?
Empagliflozin blocks SGLT2. SGLT2 is the main transporter that pulls filtered glucose back from the kidney tubule into the blood. With it blocked, less glucose returns. More glucose leaves in the urine. The label also notes it reduces sodium reabsorption. That can affect pressure inside the kidney filter and load on the heart.

The proximal tubule.
In the early part of the kidney tubule, SGLT2 sits on the cell surface. Glucose and sodium that were filtered meet it. Empagliflozin binds the transporter and slows the return of glucose to blood. The kidney threshold for glucose drops. Blood sugar can fall. The same action can reduce volume and pressure signals that matter for heart and kidney outcomes. The heart and kidney benefits are not explained by sugar loss alone.

What changes.
Blood sugar can come down in type 2 diabetes. Heart-failure hospitalization risk can fall in the labeled population. Kidney progression risk can fall in the labeled population. The effect continues while the medicine is taken. It does not cure the underlying condition.

Dose and limits.
The usual dose is 10 milligrams once a day in the morning, with or without food. For more blood-sugar control in type 2 diabetes, the dose may rise to 25 milligrams if 10 milligrams is tolerated. Do not use if you have had a serious allergic reaction to empagliflozin. Watch for ketoacidosis, even when blood sugar is not very high. Watch volume and kidney tests. Genital and urinary infections can occur because more sugar is in the urine. This film does not show your personal risk.
