## 1. What is this drug?

Welcome to Mechanism Diligence.
You might be here to understand a drug you were prescribed, or to learn how it works.
This one is empagliflozin.
Jardiance is the brand name, and it may be the name you already know.
This film follows oral tablets.

## 2. What are the labeled uses?

The label has separate plans.
One is to improve blood sugar control in type 2 diabetes, with diet and exercise.
One is to reduce the risk of cardiovascular death and hospitalization for heart failure.
One is to reduce the risk of further kidney decline, end-stage kidney disease, cardiovascular death, and hospitalization in adults with chronic kidney disease at risk of progression.
Not every person qualifies for every plan.

## 3. What does the drug block?

Empagliflozin blocks SGLT2.
SGLT2 is the main transporter that returns filtered glucose from the kidney tubule to the blood.
With it blocked, less glucose returns.
More glucose leaves in the urine.
The label also notes reduced sodium reabsorption.
The drug itself leaves mainly in urine.

## 4. Where does the work happen?

The action is in the proximal tubule of the kidney.
There, SGLT2 sits on the cell surface.
Empagliflozin binds it and slows glucose return.
The kidney threshold for glucose drops.
The same action can change pressure signals that matter for the heart and the kidney.
We are changing the scale of the picture, not watching the medicine jump.
The shapes are not the true shapes and are not to scale.

## 5. What changes?

Blood sugar can fall in type 2 diabetes.
Heart-failure hospitalization risk can fall in the labeled group.
Kidney progression risk can fall in the labeled group.
The effect continues while the medicine is taken.
It does not cure the condition.
It does not replace diet, exercise, or other labeled treatments when those are needed.

## 6. What dose does the label list?

The usual dose is 10 milligrams once a day in the morning, with or without food.
For more blood-sugar control, the dose may rise to 25 milligrams if 10 milligrams is tolerated.
These are label limits, not a dose chosen for you.

## 7. What are the limits?

Do not use it after a serious allergy to empagliflozin.
Watch for ketoacidosis, even when blood sugar is not very high.
Watch volume and kidney tests.
Genital and urinary infections can occur because more sugar is in the urine. That is the same action that lowers blood sugar.
This film is for education, not personal medical advice.
