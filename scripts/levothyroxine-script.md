## 2. What is hypothyroidism?

The thyroid gland does not make enough thyroid hormone.
Metabolism slows.
You may feel tired, cold, or gain weight.
A blood test for TSH and free T4 confirms it.
Over time, untreated low hormone can affect the heart and other systems.

Scene: thyroid gland with low output. No drug.

## 3. What does the drug do?

Levothyroxine is synthetic T4.
It is identical to the T4 the thyroid makes.
It raises T4 levels.
Most of the active hormone is T3, made from T4 in tissues.

Scene: gold T4 molecule.

## 4. Where does the work happen?

It begins as a swallowed tablet.
It is absorbed from the gut.
It circulates and enters cells.
Inside the nucleus, T4 and T3 bind thyroid receptors on DNA.
That turns on gene transcription and protein synthesis.
We are changing the scale of the picture, not watching the medicine jump.
The shapes are not the true shapes and are not to scale.

Scene: tablet, then cell, then nucleus with receptor on DNA.

## 5. What changes?

Energy use and many body processes improve while the dose is right.
The effect is measured by blood tests, not by feeling alone.
It works only while you take it.
The dose is adjusted to the individual.

Scene: metabolic activity restored.

## 6. How does it leave?

Thyroid hormones are deiodinated in tissues.
They are also conjugated and excreted into bile and urine.
The half-life of T4 is long, about 6 to 7 days.
That half-life is not how fast the dose is adjusted.

Scene: simple leave path. One picture.

## 7. What dose does the label list?

The dose is individualized.
There is no single starting or maximum dose that fits everyone.
The goal is a normal TSH for most people with primary hypothyroidism.
Take it on an empty stomach, usually in the morning, and wait before food or other medicines.
These are label principles, not a dose chosen for you.

Scene: dose principles card. No fixed numbers.

## 8. What are the limits?

Do not use it for weight loss.
There is a boxed warning against that use.
Too much can cause heart strain, bone loss, or other problems.
That excess is the same action that restores metabolism, taken too far.
