
# Rosuvastatin MOA Script — Educational Draft
# Product: Oral rosuvastatin calcium tablets (Crestor or equivalent US label). Other formulations separate.
# Scope: Adults with primary hyperlipidemia / elevated LDL-C, as adjunct to diet.

## Chapter 0: Welcome / Title
Welcome to Mechanism Diligence. You might be here to understand a drug you were prescribed, or to learn how it works. This one is rosuvastatin. Crestor is a brand name you may know. This film follows oral rosuvastatin calcium tablets for adults with high LDL cholesterol. Education only, not personal medical advice. Independent clinical review is pending.

## Chapter 1: What is high LDL cholesterol?
What is high LDL? LDL is a particle that carries cholesterol in the blood. High levels mean more of those particles. You cannot feel the number. Only a blood test confirms it. Over years high LDL can lead to buildup in artery walls.

## Chapter 2: What does rosuvastatin do?
How does rosuvastatin lower LDL? Rosuvastatin is a statin. It is a selective competitive inhibitor of HMG-CoA reductase, the enzyme that turns HMG-CoA into mevalonate, a step toward cholesterol.

## Chapter 3: The liver
Most of the action is in the liver. Liver cells take up the drug. Inside the hepatocyte, less mevalonate means less new cholesterol is made. The cell responds by putting more LDL receptors on its surface. More receptors pull more LDL out of the blood. The label also notes reduced VLDL synthesis, which means fewer particles overall.

## Chapter 4: What changes in the blood
LDL cholesterol falls. The effect can start within a week. Most of the reduction is usually seen by two to four weeks. It works while you take it daily. It does not remove existing plaque by itself.

## Chapter 5: Dose
On the US label for adults with hyperlipidemia, the usual range is 5 to 40 milligrams once a day. The maximum is 40 milligrams once a day. Some people start lower. These are label limits, not a dose chosen for you. Take it with or without food at any time of day.

## Chapter 6: Limits
Do not use if you have had a serious allergic reaction to rosuvastatin, or in active liver disease, or in pregnancy. Muscle injury can occur and is more likely at higher doses or with certain other medicines. Liver enzyme increases have been reported. This film does not show your personal risk.
